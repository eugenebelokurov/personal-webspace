This version of my website is a work in progress. I am lucky to be in the position when I don't have to advertise myself online with the goal of earning a living. Which gives me more space to share things I see, watch, read, think, create, discard on the everyday basis. This is also my another attempt to get into writing (don't want to say blogging as it somehow has a negative connotation in my head) and sharing my thoughts. There is a lot of room for improvement in how I present things verbally, hence my turning to written (typed?) medium.
